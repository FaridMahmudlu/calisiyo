- **Books, Desk Objects, Geometry Meshes**: Procedurally constructed using Three.js built-in primitives (`BoxGeometry`, `CylinderGeometry`, `OctahedronGeometry`, `TorusGeometry`, `RingGeometry`).
- **Materials & Lighting**: Standard PBR materials (`MeshStandardMaterial`) with custom HSL/HEX colors matching calisiyo design system tokens.
